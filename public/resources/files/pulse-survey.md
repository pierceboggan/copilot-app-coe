# Monthly pulse survey

Send on the first business day of each month. Keep it anonymous and under two minutes.

1. In the last month, how often did you use the Copilot app?
   - Never
   - Once or twice
   - Weekly
   - Most days

2. "The Copilot app saves me time."
   - 1 Strongly disagree
   - 2
   - 3
   - 4
   - 5 Strongly agree

3. About how many hours did it save you in a typical week? (Number)

4. Which workflow saved you the most time? (Free text)

5. What stopped you from using it more? (Free text)

6. Did the app crash, hang or lose work this month? (Yes / No. If yes, what were you doing?)

7. Besides code, what did you use it for? (Select all: research, docs, Office files, data analysis, automations, triage, presentations, other)

8. What is your role? (Engineer, PM, designer, marketing, sales, business or operations, manager, DevRel or support, security or admin, other)

Report results by team and role, never by individual.
