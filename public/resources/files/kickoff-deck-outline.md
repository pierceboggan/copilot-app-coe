# Kickoff deck outline

A 30-minute pilot kickoff. Keep the slides to 8 and spend the rest of the meeting with everyone running their first session.

1. **Why we are here.** The outcome we want to change this quarter, such as PR cycle time, and the baseline number.
2. **What the Copilot app is.** Parallel agent sessions, each isolated on its own branch. One screenshot of the session list.
3. **Three modes.** Interactive works step by step. Plan proposes first. Autopilot works on its own.
4. **Live demo (5 minutes).** Issue from My work, Plan mode, approve the plan, open the pull request.
5. **What is approved.** Repositories in scope, sandbox defaults, connectors on the approved list.
6. **What we ask of you.** Use it on real work at least three times a week. Share one win and one problem in the channel each week.
7. **Support.** Feedback channel, office hours time, your champion's name.
8. **Hands-on now.** Install, sign in and run the first-session prompt from the starter library. Champions circulate to help.

Close with the pulse survey date and the date of the first adoption review.
