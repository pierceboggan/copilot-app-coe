<h2 id="team-kit">Team kit for leads</h2>

Leading a team of engineers? Start with these four files. They get your team running several sessions at once in the first week, on code and on the work around it.

1. [Launch comms templates](/resources/files/launch-comms.md): the announcement and a week of "try this" prompts for your team channel.
2. [Starter prompt library](/resources/files/starter-prompts.md): day-one prompts, including the parallel ones.
3. [Demo day and lunch-and-learn formats](/resources/files/demo-day.md): a 45-minute session that opens with three sessions running at once.
4. [Champion training curriculum](/resources/files/champion-curriculum.md): for the person on your team who will help everyone else.

Then pick one metric from [Start from the metric](/coe/metrics/), such as PR cycle time or review turnaround, and take a baseline before you begin.
