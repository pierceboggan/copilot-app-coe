## Champion directory

Keep a list of champions so people know who to ask. Start from the [directory template](/resources/files/champion-directory.csv) and publish it somewhere everyone can read, such as your intranet or a pinned post in the feedback channel.

Each row lists the team, the champion, a backup, their time zone, the main repositories and an office hours slot.

## Office hours

Hold 30 minutes every week, at a time that rotates across time zones if you need it.

| Time | Segment |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00 | One new tip or workflow, shown live |
| 0:05 | Bring your problem: attendees share their screen and work through it with a champion |
| 0:20 | Reliability round: crashes, hangs or lost work from the past week, with the time and what the person was doing |
| 0:25 | Wrap up: log open issues and owners |

The champion who runs the session writes up the notes and files reliability reports. Use **Share feedback** in the app, or open an issue in the public [github/app repository](https://github.com/github/app/issues) with the app version, operating system and steps to reproduce. Attach the report from the app's **Health check** when sign-in or startup fails. Issues in that repository are public, so leave out internal code, data and names.

## Feedback loop

1. **Collect.** One feedback channel in Teams or Slack, plus the monthly [pulse survey](/resources/files/pulse-survey.md).
2. **Triage.** The CoE lead tags each item: how-to question, policy blocker, product bug or idea.
3. **Act.** How-to questions go into the FAQ. Policy blockers go to the admin partner. Product bugs and ideas go to GitHub through your account team or the in-app feedback.
4. **Close the loop.** Post a short "you asked, we did" note every month.

An automation can do the first pass. Point a weekly automation at the feedback channel through an approved connector and have it draft the triage table for the CoE lead to review.

## Share wins

Ask each champion for one story a month: the task, the prompt, the time it saved. Collect them in a shared document and use the best ones in launch comms and demo day.
