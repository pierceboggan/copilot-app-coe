A Center of Excellence (CoE) is a small group that owns adoption of the GitHub Copilot app. It sets defaults, builds shared assets, trains people and measures results. Most organizations start with a lead and a few champions, then grow the champion network as more teams join.

Work through the [rollout checklist](/coe/checklist/) to track progress phase by phase.

## Charter

Write a one-paragraph charter and share it with the sponsor. A good charter answers four questions:

1. Which teams and repositories are in scope this quarter?
2. What outcome are you trying to change, such as PR cycle time or time to first commit?
3. Who decides policy questions like cloud automation tools and sandbox defaults?
4. How and when will you report progress?

## Roles

| Role | Who | Responsibilities | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Executive sponsor** | Director or VP | Sets the goal, funds time, clears blockers, attends the monthly review | 1 hour a month |
| **CoE lead** | Senior engineer, EM or developer productivity owner | Runs the program, owns the checklist, reports metrics, runs office hours | 20 to 50 percent |
| **Champions** | One per team | Demos, first-line support, prompt library and repository config upkeep | 2 to 4 hours a week |
| **Admin** | GitHub organization or enterprise owner | Seats, policies and settings for cloud agent, automations and remote control | As needed |
| **Security partner** | AppSec or platform security | Reviews repository configuration practices, sandbox defaults and automation tool scopes | As needed |

### Responsibility matrix

R is responsible, A is accountable, C is consulted and I is informed.

| Activity | Sponsor | Lead | Champions | Admin | Security |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Set goals and scope | A | R | C | I | I |
| Seats and policies | I | C | I | A/R | C |
| Repository configuration | I | A | R | I | C |
| Prompt library | I | A | R | I | I |
| Automation tool scopes | I | C | R | C | A |
| Metrics and reporting | I | A/R | C | C | I |

## Cadence

| Meeting | Who | When | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Office hours | Lead and champions, open to all | Weekly, 30 minutes | Live help, demos of new workflows |
| Champion sync | Lead and champions | Every two weeks | Share patterns, update the prompt library, surface blockers |
| Adoption review | Sponsor, lead, champions | Monthly | Review metrics, decide next month's focus |
| Maturity check | Lead | Quarterly | Revisit the checklist and record each team's phase |

## Governance

Settle policy before the pilot grows: repository configuration review, automation tool scopes, sandbox defaults, connector approval and a ring-based rollout. The [governance guide](/coe/governance/) covers each one. [Customizations and plugin marketplaces](/coe/customizations/) covers shipping instructions, skills and plugins to everyone and controlling them with enterprise-managed settings.

## Measure what matters

Track the activation funnel from awareness to weekly use, plus delivery and quality outcomes against a baseline. The [measurement guide](/coe/measurement/) has the metrics, a pulse survey and an ROI worksheet.

## Tools for the CoE team

- [Maturity assessment](/coe/assessment/): ten questions that point you to a phase.
- [Rollout checklist](/coe/checklist/): track progress and export it to Markdown or a GitHub issue.
- [Playbooks](/playbooks/): week-by-week plans by organization size.
- [Resource library](/resources/): admin briefing, launch comms, training and measurement templates.
